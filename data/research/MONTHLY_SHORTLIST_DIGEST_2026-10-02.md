## Limits and next rotation

This was a targeted monthly rotation, not a complete audit of all 1,389 records. The largest open risk remains the unreviewed long tail and generic short names such as ARJN, MHR, Makan, DKP, EFY and Gabri. High Google volume for a short term is not identity proof. Next month's research should prioritize those identities plus high-volume unreviewed comedy and Punjabi records, using post-event evidence rather than ticket listings alone.
